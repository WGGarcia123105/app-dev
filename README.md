My Favorite Series: When Life Gives You Tangerines

When Life Gives You Tangerines is a heartwarming, sweeping Korean period drama directed by Kim Won-seok. It's one of those stories that leaves you deeply moved long after the final episode.

### Plot Summary:

The series follows *Oh Ae-sun*, a rebellious aspiring poet born into poverty, and *Yang Gwan-sik*, an iron-willed, loyal young man who silently loves her. Spanning four seasons across multiple decades on Jeju Island, the story traces their lifelong romance, individual dreams, and overcoming generational hardships.

### Key Characters:

* *Oh Ae-sun* – Played by IU (young) & Moon So-ri (middle-aged), she is a bold, ambitious girl fighting adversity to pursue literature.
* *Yang Gwan-sik* – Played by Park Bo-gum (young) & Park Hae-joon (middle-aged), Cobb's counterpart in unwavering devotion and Ae-sun's lifelong anchor.
* *Yang Geum-myeong* – Played by IU, Ae-sun and Gwan-sik’s daughter who navigates modern life in Seoul while narrating her parents' story.

*"The title refers to making something sweet and comforting out of the sour tangerines life hurls at you."*
— IU


---

### Themes:

* *Resilience vs. Hardship* – The boundary between enduring life's severe challenges and finding quiet joy in love.
* *Generational Love and Sacrifice* – A daughter's journey of discovering her parents' past, sacrifices, and unspoken devotion.

---

### Trivia:

* The Korean title Pokssak Sogatsuda is written in Jeju dialect, meaning "Thank you for your hard work."
* The 16-episode series was released globally on Netflix in four seasonal volume acts: Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter.

---

### Why I Love It:

When Life Gives You Tangerines is a masterpiece that mixes romance, historical depth, and emotional resonance. It's the type of story you can revisit multiple times and still find new meaning in its warmth and spectacular cinematography.
